## 1. Character name check

### 1.1 Summary

| Name | Rating | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Pista** (hero) | **CAUTION (low)** | No live "PISTA" word mark found in classes 9/28/41 and no game character called Pista. But it is an everyday word in Italian, Spanish and Portuguese ("track", "clue", "dance floor"), it is used in a PC game title (PISTA Motorsport) and inside Ferrari's "FERRARI 488 PISTA" mark (classes 12 and 28). Fine as a character name; weak and crowded as a stand-alone brand. |
| **Duko** (macaw) | **CLEAR (minor notes)** | No game, toy or entertainment use found apart from a tiny 2019 game-jam prototype. Existing "DUKO" uses are apparel/retail (class 25 etc.). It sounds a little like "Dooku" (Star Wars villain, mark "COUNT DOOKU", class 28) and "Duki" (Argentine rapper); spelling, meaning and context differ. |

Neither name is a conflict. Neither name has an embarrassing meaning in the seven languages checked.

### 1.2 Pista: evidence

Registers and products (classes 9 software/games, 28 toys, 41 entertainment):
- No "PISTA" word mark in classes 9, 28 or 41 surfaced in searches of indexed USPTO data. The closest US filing
  found is **PISTA BIKES** (serial 97217372, class 12 bicycles, filed 2022):
  [trademark.justia.com/972/17/pista-97217372.html](https://trademark.justia.com/972/17/pista-97217372.html).
- **FERRARI 488 PISTA** (US reg. 5964032, registered 2020-01-21, classes 12 and 28, stylised word mark):
  [trademark.justia.com/792/53/ferrari-488-79253252.html](https://trademark.justia.com/792/53/ferrari-488-79253252.html).
  Class 28 covers toys and games. The mark is the whole phrase, and Ferrari lost an EU appeal on this mark in
  2025 where the court treated "pista" as an Italian word German consumers would not necessarily understand
  ([Law360 summary](https://www.law360.co.uk/articles/2409931)). Ferrari is known for defending model names
  (Testarossa case). Risk to a *character* called Pista in a jungle game is low; risk rises if "Pista" is used
  alone as the app name next to racing/track imagery.
- **PISTA Motorsport**: a sim-racing game on PC (Steam early access) by REG Simulations, Argentina. A class 9 use of
  the word as a game title. No registration found.
  ([overtake.gg](https://overtake.gg/news/comments/91368), [traxion.gg](https://traxion.gg/argentinian-sim-racing-title-pista-motorsport-hits-pc-early-access))
- App Store: many "Pişti"/"Pisti" card games and Spanish word games titled "Pistas" (= "clues"), for example
  [Pistas](https://apps.apple.com/mx/app/pistas/id6447576430), [Pişti Online](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1436761586).
  None is a runner or uses Pista as a character. This makes "Pista" alone a crowded, low-distinctiveness app name.
- Characters: no game/cartoon character named Pista found. Near misses: **Pisti**, a girl in the anime *Magi* who
  talks to animals ([MyAnimeList](https://myanimelist.net/character/54595)); **Pakista** in Puyo Puyo
  ([Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Characters/Pakista)). Different names; worth knowing because Pisti is also a
  girl with animal friends. Keep Pista's look and story clearly our own (already a style-guide rule).

Meanings and slang:

"Pista" itself does not mean alcohol. | Low. One letter from an alcohol slang word in Mexican Spanish; fine as a name, but do not pair it with drink imagery or wordplay. |
| Italian | "track, racetrack, runway"; "pista!" = "make way!" | None (fits the runner) |

### 1.3 Duko: evidence

Registers and products:
- No "DUKO" mark in classes 9, 28 or 41 found. Searches return unrelated near marks (e.g. "D DUCKO", toys-related
  design codes, reg. 3466239: [justia](https://trademark.justia.com/772/78/d-77278925.html)).
- Existing uses of the word: **Duko International** (Chinese apparel and bags, class 25/18 territory)
  ([made-in-china](https://m.made-in-china.com/company-dukobag/)); "Duko" S.T. Dupont retail stores in Korea
  ([ktriptips](https://www.ktriptips.com/eng/shopping/2956387)). Different goods.
- Games: a five-level Sokoban-style game-jam prototype called "Duko" on itch.io (about 2019, inactive)
  ([itch.io](https://itch.io/post/8248707)). No App Store or Google Play game or character called Duko found.
- No parrot or bird character called Duko found ([search of cartoon parrots](https://parrotfunzone.com/explore-parrots/cartoon-parrots)).
- Sound-alikes: **COUNT DOOKU** (Lucasfilm, US reg. 2840628, class 28 toys, renewed)
  ([justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/761/21/count-76121325.html)); Lucasfilm registers even minor character
  names. "Duko" (DOO-koh) vs "Dooku" (DOO-koo): similar sound, different spelling, different meaning, and a parrot
  vs a Sith lord, so confusion is unlikely. Also "Duki", a well-known Argentine rapper. Do not pronounce or style
  the name "Dooku" in voice lines or marketing.

Meanings and slang: English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, French, German, Polish: **no meaning found.** Extra:
Tagalog/Cebuano "dukô" = bent, stooped, shy; Esperanto "duko" = duke
([tagalog.com](https://www.tagalog.com/dictionary/duko), [babelfree](https://babelfree.com/dictionary/eo/meaning-of-duko)). Harmless.

Originality watch (not a trademark issue): a **sly, greedy macaw sidekick** is also the personality of Disney's
Iago (Aladdin, red macaw). Duko's violet/orange palette already avoids the look; keep the voice direction away from
Iago's (no rasping, wise-cracking villain-henchman delivery) and never use red as Duko's main color.

### 1.4 When a lawyer check is worth it

A web search is not clearance. A trademark attorney's **clearance search** (full register search in the US, EU and
UK, plus common-law and app-store use, with an opinion) is worth paying for:
1. **Before the public app name is locked** (App Store Connect app record name, store art, icon text, trailer).
   This matters more than the character names because the app name is the brand.
2. **Before voice recordings and store art bake in "Pista" or "Duko"** (planned week 5). The GDD currently places the
   trademark check in week 7 (section 22); that is after the recordings. **Recommendation: move it to week 3.**
3. **If the owner wants to register** the app name or character names as trademarks (likely classes 9, 28, 41).
   "Pista" alone would be hard to register in the EU because it is a dictionary word in Italian/Spanish/Portuguese;
   a coined app name (e.g. "Swingbound") registers more easily.
4. Immediately if anyone sends a complaint, or if merchandise or a brand deal is planned.

## 2. Public app name

Rules applied: ≤ 30 characters each (Guideline 2.3.7); no "Booze" or alcohol reference; no third-party trademarks
(no Tarzan, Temple Run, Subway Surfers, Jungle Book/Mowgli, Rio); no "for kids" (2.3.8, 5.1.4); name must be unique
on the App Store. ASO: Apple indexes name, subtitle and the 100-character keyword field; the character names have
no search volume yet, so "jungle", "swing" and "runner" must appear in name or subtitle, without repeating words
across the three fields.

Shared note on "Vine": **VINE** is a live US registration for video-sharing app software (reg. 4463660, class 9,
renewed; [docketalarm](https://www.docketalarm.com/trademarks/85713066/VINE)). Using "vine"/"vines" to describe jungle
vines in a game is descriptive use and low risk, but it is a reason to prefer names where "Vine" is not the lead word.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pista & Duko: Jungle Swing** (26) | Endless vine-swinging runner (28) | No app or mark with this full name found. "Jungle Swing" alone is a generic browser-game title, so the character names carry the uniqueness. Strong search words (jungle, swing, runner). | Clear |
| 2 | **Swingbound: Pista & Duko** (24) | Jungle runner: swing and fly (28) | No game, app or mark called "Swingbound" found ([search](https://trademarks.justia.com/778/92/swingswing-77892325.html) returned only unrelated SWING marks). Coined word: the most distinctive and easiest to protect; weaker search value, so the subtitle does the ASO work. | Clear |
| 3 | **Pista: Swing the Jungle** (23) | Endless runner, macaw sidekick (30) | No match found. Drops Duko from the name. | Clear |
| 4 | **Pista & Duko: Treasure Vines** (28) | Swing, dash and grab the gold (29) | No match found. Sells the treasure-hunt mood; "Vines" note above. | Clear |
| 5 | **Pista & Duko: Vine Swing** (24) | Endless jungle runner (21) | No exact match. Vine-swing games exist under other names (Swingo!, Monkey Hook, Benji Bananas). "Vine" note above. | Clear (low caution) |
| 6 | **Pista: Vine Swing Runner** (24) | Leap the gaps with Duko (23) | No exact match; generic, reads like a keyword string and is the least memorable. | Caution |
| 7 | **Pista's Vine Leap** (17) | Jungle runner with a macaw pal (30) | "Vine Leap" is an itch.io browser prototype ([itch.io](https://arajani1.itch.io/vine-leap)). Small, but a same-genre title. | Caution |
| 8 | **Duko & Pista: Jungle Leap** (25) | Swing vines, outrun the ruins (29) | "Jungle Leap" is a fan game inspired by Gorilla Tag ([itch.io](https://c3lcius.itch.io/jungle-leap)); association with another game's community is unwanted. | Caution |

Dropped while drafting: **"Vinebound: Pista's Run"**: conflict with *Vinebound: Tangled Together* (Silesia Games,
Switch and PlayStation, 2025) ([nintendo.com](https://www.nintendo.com/games/detail/vinebound-tangled-together-switch)).

Recommendation: rank 1 for the store (best balance of search value and safety), with rank 2 as the fallback if the
owner wants a protectable brand word. Either way, the attorney clearance search (1.4) should run on the chosen
name before it goes into App Store Connect.

Keyword field starter (EN-US, ≤ 100 chars, no words repeated from rank 1's name/subtitle, no trademarks):
`parrot,macaw,adventure,treasure,dash,jump,ruins,river,mountain,arcade,offline,gold,chase` (88 chars; final list
at week 7 with real ASO data). EN-GB, EN-CA and EN-AU listings each get their own keyword field; use them for
spelling and synonym variants rather than copies. Never use competitor or third-party names ("temple", "subway",
"tarzan", "jungle book", "rio") in any field (2.3.7, 4.1(c)).

## 3. Early-risk review (GDD fourth draft)

Severity: **High** = likely rejection or legal exposure if not fixed; **Med** = possible rejection or unwanted
rating/label; **Low** = polish or future-proofing.

| # | Risk | Where | Guideline / rule | Sev. | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Working title leaks into user-visible places.** "JungleBooze" could end up as the Unity product name, home-screen name (CFBundleDisplayName), splash/title text, bundle ID, IAP product IDs, Game Center IDs, or the privacy-policy/support URL (e.g. a repo-named web page). A "Booze" URL on the store page undoes the name decision. | Unity Player Settings (not created yet), store URLs | 2.3, 2.3.8, GDD 17.1 | High | Choose a neutral bundle ID and ID prefix **before** the App Store Connect record is created (bundle ID and product IDs can never be changed or reused). | R2 | **Kid hero + cartoon style + ads = "directed to children" risk.** Apple: apps primarily for kids should not have third-party analytics or ads (5.1.4).
