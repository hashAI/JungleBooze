## How to read this
- **Form:** "Aurelia: <coined word>", all ≤ 30 characters (2.3.7 limit). Every option leaves room for a later
  sequel/season suffix.
- **Distinctiveness comes from the coined word.** "Aurelia" carries little weight on its own. The App Store already has
  *Aurelia: AI Language Tutor*, *Aurelia: AI Wedding Photoshoot*, *Aurelia AR*, *Aurelia Gem Chamber* and
  *Kingdom of Aurelia: Adventure* (search API, 2026-10-09). So the coined word must be clean and memorable by itself.
- **Brief fit:** evokes exploration, wonder, and an endless living world; original (Blueprint Part II); easy to say and
  spell in English (launch language: English); no "Run"/"Runner" (4.3(b) note); no "Jungle" (the world is an
  original alien ecosystem, and "Jungle" pulls toward Temple Run comparisons).
- **Risk:** **L** = no conflicting game/app/mark found; **M** = a similar-sounding game/app or mark exists in a nearby
  class, or a weak element; **H** = an identical or near-identical game mark (none on this list; screened out).
- **Domains:** every bare `.com` is already registered (normal for short coined words). The combined
  `aurelia<word>.com` was **unregistered for all 8** on 2026-10-09 (WHOIS). Social handles were **not** checked; do a
  manual check (X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Discord) once the owner shortlists.

## Subtitles for the top 3 (≤ 30 characters)

Rules applied: 2.3.7 (no pricing words, so **no "free"** even in "free steering", because a reviewer may read it as a price
claim; no reference to other apps, so **no "no lanes"**; no unverifiable claims like "best"). Words already in the name are
not repeated, so each subtitle adds new search terms. Each subtitle leads with the mechanics that answer 4.3(b): steering anywhere,
route choice, discovery.

| Name | Subtitle (recommended) | Chars | Alternate | Chars |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aurelia: Wildward | **Steer anywhere. Choose a path.** | 30 | Steer, pick routes, discover | 28 |
| Aurelia: Lumenwild | **Steer anywhere. Find secrets.** | 29 | Pick a path. Find the secrets. | 30 |
| Aurelia: Verdaway | **Pick a path. Find the secrets.** | 30 | Steer, pick routes, discover | 28 |

All subtitles must stay true in the shipped build: "find secrets" and "choose a path" require the secret routes and
forks from the MVP (Blueprint Part LIII) to be in the reviewed build (2.3.1 accurate metadata).

## Next steps
1. The owner picks 1–2 names (with a fallback). The coordinator logs the choice in `design/DECISIONS.md`.
2. Register `aurelia<word>.com` (about $10–15/yr, so it needs the owner's spend OK) and reserve the social handles the same day.
3. Attorney search for the chosen name plus a fallback (US/EU/UK, classes 9, 28, 41), and include "Pista" (see the design
   review). Do this before any logo or store art.
4. Reserve the name in App Store Connect when the app record is created. A name reservation does not create trademark rights.
