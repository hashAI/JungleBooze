## Blocking

### B1. A swipe after the thumb has rested is lost or late (AC-101-32 fails in the core grip)
- **Where:** `Scripts/Gameplay/Controls/GestureRecognizer.cs:257-269` (`DetectSwipe` breaks on the first sample older than
  the window).
- **Problem:** iOS and Enhanced Touch send no samples while a finger is still. After a rest longer than 0.12 s, the
  last sample (where the finger rested) is older than `windowStart`. The loop then `break`s before using it as the
  reference. So the distance covered by the first moving sample of the swipe is never counted.
- **Failure scenario:** the steering thumb rests 0.5 s, then swipes up 30 pt in 67 ms (450 pt/s; 60 Hz samples at
  7.5/15/22.5/30 pt). The largest in-window displacement is 22.5 pt, below 24, so **no Jump**. The spec requires a
  Jump: 24 pt within 120 ms. Longer swipes fire one sample late (+8–17 ms). In Steer + Flick, the thumb rests on the
  glass most of the time, so this is the common case. It would show up as "didn't register" reports (M8).
- **Fix:** in the loop, when a sample is older than `windowStart`, still evaluate it once as the reference, because the
  finger was still at that position until the next sample. Then break. Add tests: Begin, hold 0.5 s, swipe 30 pt in
  4 samples → Jump. Repeat at 120 Hz.

## Should fix

### S1. A dodge after a drag the other way throws Pista up to 4.4 m (spec formula defect)
- **Where:** `Scripts/Gameplay/Movement/RunnerSimulation.cs:578-586`. Implements spec §2.3 exactly.
- **Problem:** `made = dir·(xT − origin)` is negative when the gesture first went the other way, so
  `shift = max(2.2, made)` is measured from the touch origin, not from where Pista is.
- **Failure scenario:** drag right 2.0 m (50 pt) around a rock, then release-flick left (default on, ≥1,200 pt/s).
  The new `xT` is origin − 2.2, which is **4.2 m left of the current target**. That can carry her into a blocker or the
  edge she was avoiding. A second touch's `TouchBegan` also resets the origin for touch A's gesture
  (`GestureRecognizer.cs:222`).
- **Fix (needs game-designer):** when `dir·(xT − origin) < 0`, use the current `xT` as the origin. Alternatively,
  reset `DodgeOriginX` on each lateral direction reversal of the steering touch. Make `TouchBegan` per-touch. Add an
  AC and a test.

### S2. Swipes between 30° and 35° steer and jump; noisy real swipes leak steering
- **Where:** `GestureRecognizer.cs:232`, which filters steering per sample at 30°, while swipes are accepted up to 35°.
- **Failure scenario:** a 33° swipe up of 24 pt passes about 15.6 pt of lateral, which is **0.62 m of steering**, and
  also jumps. Curved or noisy real swipes (per-sample angle above 30° at the start) leak steering the same way.
  AC-101-33 only tests a perfectly straight synthetic line.
- **Fix:** when a vertical swipe fires, subtract the lateral that the window's samples added (keep a per-sample
  lateral history). Or route the 30/35° mismatch to game-designer. Add jittered-sample tests.

### S3. Focus loss does not pause; the resume countdown survives backgrounding
- **Where:** `Scripts/App/FeelTest/FeelTestRoot.cs:279-285`.
- **Problem:** only `OnApplicationPause(true)` pauses. Control Center, Notification Center, Siri and call banners send
  `OnApplicationFocus(false)`. If the app backgrounds during the 1 s countdown (`_paused` is already true), the
  countdown continues on return and the run restarts without a full ready beat (spec §9).
- **Fix:** also pause on `OnApplicationFocus(false)`. On any pause or focus loss, reset `_resumeCountdown = 0` so the
  player must resume again. Verify on device.

### S4. A 180° landscape rotation does not cancel touches
- **Where:** `FeelTestRoot.cs:502-517`. Touches are cancelled only on landscape↔portrait. ProjectSettings allow
  LandscapeLeft↔Right.
- **Failure scenario:** the phone flips while the thumb is down. Screen coordinates mirror, and the next Moved sample
  jumps up to about 800 pt, which is **32 m of target** (slammed to the edge) and can also trigger a release-flick
  dodge. Spec §5 says active touches are cancelled on an orientation change.
- **Fix:** track `Screen.orientation` as well and call `CancelAll` when it changes. Device-verify.

### S5. Any floor rise is walked up; `StepUpHeight` is dead config
- **Where:** `RunnerSimulation.cs:699-703`. `JumpSlideConfig.StepUpHeight` is never read.
- **Problem:** spec §2.4 / AC-101-21 limit step-up to 0.35 m. The code teleports onto any rise (comment
  `[ASSUMED]`). This is not logged in `docs/STATUS.md` or the spec.
- **Failure scenario:** a chunk with a 1.0 m ledge (spec 102) gets snapped up in one tick. Visual pop, and the tuning
  value in the asset does nothing.
- **Fix:** either honour `StepUpHeight` (treat a higher rise as a wall/Blocker in chunk authoring) or get a spec
  amendment and delete the field. Add a test for a 0.5 m rise.

### S6. ADR 0006 was self-accepted
- **Where:** `docs/adr/0006-steering-input-format.md:3-5`. It says "Accepted" and also "tech-architect to confirm at
  review". The ADR is marked hard to undo.
- **Fix:** set it to Proposed until tech-architect signs off.

### S7. The AC-101-44 visibility test is weak and not in PlayMode
- **Where:** `Tests/EditMode/CameraRig/CameraRigTests.cs:202-246`.
- **Problem:** it checks one point per obstacle (centre clamped to ±3.5) at a single tick, using the math rig and not
  the real `Camera`. It runs the safe branch only, so risky-branch obstacles are seen only from the left. Only 19.5:9
  aspects are tested. It ignores occlusion (the 3.2 m divider can hide right-branch content).
- **Fix:** PlayMode test with the real camera. Check all 8 box corners, or require ≥ 50% of the face. Cover the risky
  branch and 16:9. Add a raycast occlusion check.

### S8. The zero-allocation check does not cover the real frame
- **Where:** `Tests/PlayMode/FeelTestPlayModeTests.cs:45-102` covers sim, recognizer, dispatcher and bot only.
- **Problem:** ARCHITECTURE §10.1 says 0 B per frame during a run. The following are untested: `FeelTestRoot.Update`
  → `UnityPointerInput`, `RunnerView`, `CameraRigModel`, HUD and `DrainEvents`. Known allocation:
  `InputRecording` (`FeelTestRoot.cs:147`) starts at 4,096 entries. A slow run with continuous steering (more than
  68 s of Running) grows the List mid-run.
- **Fix:** add a PlayMode allocation test that drives `FeelTestRoot` for 600 frames with synthetic touches. Size the
  recording from the course length (for example `FinishS / v0 · 60 · 1.25`).

### S9. Gesture tests only use clean 60 Hz samples
- **Where:** `Tests/EditMode/Controls/GestureRecognizerTests.cs:11`.
- **Problem:** ProMotion devices deliver 120 Hz touches (240 Hz is possible). There are no tests for jitter, curved
  paths, or held-then-swipe (this is what hid B1).
- **Fix:** parameterize the stroke helper by sample rate (60/120/240) and by noise (±1 pt), and rerun AC-101-32…37.

## Nits

1. `RunSession.cs:103` + `FeelTestRoot.cs:332`: lateral input during Finishing is applied but not recorded, so replays
   diverge after the finish (cosmetic). Record every phase, or ignore lateral input in Finishing.
2. `FeelTestRoot.cs:583,608`: the debug overlay boxes enums (`Append(_session.Phase)`) and concatenates strings every
   0.1 s while visible. These are allocations in a debug build; use cached names.
3. `CourseView.cs:86-98`: this is a second per-frame `Update`, while ARCHITECTURE §4 says the root is the only one.
   It rotates every coin, including collected ones, and keeps spinning while paused. Drive it from `Present`.
4. `PerfectBot.cs:122-157`: planning can run about 90 × 240 probe steps in one tick. Toggling bot autoplay (B) on a
   device spikes frames. Keep the bot out of M7 measurements, or plan incrementally.
5. `ScreenPointScale.cs:28`: the dpi heuristic is about 4% off on downsampled minis (2.88 px/pt) and 15% off on
   Plus models. Document this, or derive the scale from a native plugin later.
6. `RunnerSimulation.cs:283-295`: ground and gap checks use the s before advancing (the spec's order), so leaving and
   landing are detected one tick late. Coyote is effectively 7 ticks from the lip. Document it in the spec.
7. `RunnerSimulation.cs:936`: an obstacle touched during i-frames is marked resolved, so long thorn patches and
   blockers stay ghostable afterwards. This follows the spec, but spec 102 authoring should know.
8. `RunnerSimulation.cs:650-657`: `EdgeBrush` fires every tick while settling within 5 cm of the edge, even without an
   outward push (`|vDes| > 0.01`). The view should rate-limit it.
9. `FrameInputDispatcher.cs:96`: keyboard commands wait until the gesture queue is empty, so mixed sources are not in
   recognition order. Also, `CommandQueue.Dropped` is never shown in the overlay.
10. `GestureRecognizer.cs:280`: one long upward swipe of 0.25 s or more re-fires Jump, which is buffered and becomes a
    surprise jump on landing. This follows the spec (re-arm 0.18 s); flag it in the playtest.
11. `CameraRigModel.cs:127-128`: landing on a log changes `GroundY` (half-life 0.3) and air height (half-life 0.2)
    together, so the camera sags slightly then rises. Blend `GroundY + air` on one spring.
12. `RunnerSimulation.cs:351-354`: redundant floor lookup in `Revive`. If the safe point is over a gap, Pista is set
    Grounded at y = 0 with no floor. `Revive` also does not snap the camera rig.
13. Tuning literals in code: `RunnerView.cs:13` (`BlinkHz` = 8, a spec value), `RunnerSimulation.cs:20`
    (`SafeSampleSpacing`). Move them to config.
14. `ProjectSettings.asset:94-95`: deferring system gestures on all edges and hiding the home indicator are global
    settings, but the spec says "run screen". Fine for Phase 1; revisit when menus exist.
    `CameraRigTests.cs:251` logs Pista's screen fraction but does not assert the 14–18% target.
