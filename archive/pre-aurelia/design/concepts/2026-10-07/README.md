# Concept images, 2026-10-07: Pista (H2 "Mapcloth") and Duko (M3 "Dusk")

**Owner:** art-director | **Purpose:** pick one hero take and one macaw take as the input for the image-to-3D test
(Meshy vs Tripo). | **Status:** waiting for the owner's pick (see "Open questions" at the end).

All images: OpenAI Images API, model `gpt-image-2`, `quality: high`, `n: 1`. The API has no seed or negative-prompt
parameter, so no seed is logged and the "Avoid:" sentence at the end of each prompt replaces the negative prompt
(global negative + character negative from `design/prompts/`, shortened). The SUBJECT blocks are word-for-word the locked
blocks in `design/prompts/hero_variants.md` and `macaw_variants.md`, and STYLE is `{STYLE_C_CHAR}` word-for-word. Only the
FORMAT block, a "Rendering / coverage" clause and a per-take "Interpretation" sentence differ. No character names appear in any prompt.

Hex color swatches and head-height guide lines from the template model sheet were left out on purpose: the
generator renders them as text and numbers, which pollutes image-to-3D input.

Budget: 12 of 30 generations used (hero 7, macaw 3, key art 2).

## Hero (H2 "Mapcloth")

### Iterations (why the prompt grew)
1. **v1** (take 1 only, `rejected/hero_take1_v1_painterly.jpg`): good design, but painterly rather than cel-shaded, sash
   at the waist, satchel on her **left** hip. Fix: added a "Rendering" clause (cel-shaded toon render, heavy ink outline,
   no brush texture) and sash/satchel placement.
2. **v2** (all three takes, `rejected/*_v2_*`): the generator turned the wrap top into a strapless bandeau with a bare
   midriff, and in takes 1 and 3 printed the X on bare skin (it reads as a tattoo, which goes against the design and the "no
   tribal tattoo" rule, and the bare midriff isn't age-appropriate). The satchel was still on her left hip. Fix: added a
   "Clothing coverage" sentence and spelled out the satchel side in image terms.
3. **v3** (final, below): all three takes pass the locked design checks.
